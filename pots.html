Privacy Policy

POTS Tracker

Last updated: June 19, 2026

1. Data Collection

POTS Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you enter in the app is stored exclusively on your device.

2. Data Storage

All health data (symptoms, measurements, notes) is stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database. No data is sent to external servers.

3. No Third-Party Services

The free trial version of the app does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No third parties have access to your data.

4. Subscription

Subscription management is handled by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Payment data is processed exclusively by Google or Apple. We do not store payment information.

5. Health Data

Health information you enter is used solely to display it back to you within the app and to generate your personal PDF report. This data never leaves your device.

6. Children

This app is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: ngzlegacy.support@pm.me
